Find your fivefold "calling"

You will see 20 pairs of statements. Tap the one that sounds more like you. You get 10 seconds per pair, so go with your gut.

At the end you get your top two letters: Apostle, Prophet, Evangelist, Shepherd or Teacher.

Built for a class presentation. It copies the format of the commercial APEST test (forced-choice pairs on a timer) but is not the official 5Q assessment and has no scientific validity. Neither, as far as anyone has published, does the original. Nothing you tap leaves this page.
